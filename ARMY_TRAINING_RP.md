# Army Training RP

Purpose

This document contains the rules and procedures for hosting and participating in Army Training roleplay sessions. It should be used alongside the main Staff Moderation Handbook.

Attendance & Equipment

- All participants must join the Police Department before training begins.
- Participants must equip the **Recruit Army PT Uniform** with no accessories that look unrealistic.
- Participants must be armed with a firearm (no pistols allowed).

Staging Area & Transport

- Participants may NOT spawn any vehicles during staging or training.
- Participants must wait at the main entrance (the entrance that leads to the side near the meeting room).
- Participants must neatly line up and must not cut in line at the side entrance while transport arrives.
- During any bus/transport ride to HRP, participants must remain seated and must not jump out of their seats.
- Only staff members are allowed inside the transport convoy; no civilians or non-staff participants may be inside convoy vehicle(s).

Conduct During PT

- PT must always be enabled during training sessions.
- Participants must follow staff instruction at all times.
- Do not teleport to random staff members during the training session — this risks receiving a strike.
- Failure to follow staff instructions can result in strikes and/or immediate enforcement actions depending on severity.

Moderator Enforcement

Moderators are required to enforce these rules during training sessions. Recommended responses:

- Spawning vehicles during training: jail or kick depending on severity and intent.
- Any other training offences: enforce proportionally (warning → kick → jail → ban) based on severity and repeat behavior.
- Cutting in line or disrespect to staff/members: issue a warning; escalate if repeated.

Hosting Permissions & Restrictions

- Only staff explicitly authorized may host an Army Training session.
- If you are not authorized, do not start or advertise training sessions.

Teleportation & Strike Policy

- Do not teleport to random staff members or other players during training sessions.
- Strikes are applied when participants intentionally disobey staff instructions or disrupt training:
  - 1st strike: Warning

Note: Further strikes or severe infractions may lead to kicks, jail time, or bans at staff discretion.

PM Templates (examples)

- Warning (minor):
  - `:pm Player123 You have received a WARNING for cutting the line during Army Training. Please follow staff instructions. Continued offenses may lead to further action.`

- Kick (major / repeated):
  - `:pm Player123 You have been KICKED for spawning vehicles during Army Training (30 minutes). Appeal to SeniorStaff with evidence.`

- Jail / Ban (severe):
  - `:pm Player123 You have been JAILED/BANNED for [offense]. Evidence has been recorded. Contact SeniorStaff to appeal.`

Recordkeeping & Reporting

- Staff should record incidents (usernames, time, offense, evidence) when issuing enforcement for training sessions.
- Use the main handbook's incident/reporting process or the designated incident log file in the repository if available.

Questions & Changes

- If training rules need to be updated, submit suggested changes to the staff lead or create an issue in the repository for review.
