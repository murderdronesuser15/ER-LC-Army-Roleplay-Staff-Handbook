# Staff Moderation Handbook

Welcome to the Army Roleplay Moderation Team!

We are excited to have you as a part of our staff team. Becoming a staff member means you are trusted with the responsibility of helping maintain a professional, fair, and enjoyable environment for everyone in our community.

This handbook was created to provide staff members with the information and resources needed to properly moderate the server, handle situations professionally, and understand the expectations that come with being part of the ARP staff team.

Inside this handbook, you will find information regarding:

- Moderation commands
- Punishment procedures
- Staff expectations
- Roleplay standards
- Roleplay operations
- Convoy procedures
- Staff professionalism

All staff members are expected to read and understand this handbook before actively moderating.

## Staff Expectations

As a member of the ARP Staff Team, you represent the community whenever you are on duty. Your actions, communication, and decisions reflect the reputation of the entire staff team.

Staff members are expected to:

- Remain professional at all times
- Treat players fairly and respectfully
- Use moderation powers responsibly
- Follow the punishment guidelines
- Avoid abusing commands or permissions
- Communicate properly with players and other staff members

Staff powers are a responsibility, not a privilege to be used for personal reasons.

## Command Usage Guidelines

When using moderation commands, always ensure you are selecting the correct player before executing the command.

Examples of moderation commands include:

- :bring
- :kill
- :kick

Other staff commands

Most commands can be executed by entering approximately the first three letters of a player's username.

If you accidentally use a command on the wrong player, you are expected to correct the situation immediately.

Never use commands unnecessarily or for personal benefit.

## Punishment Guidelines

The punishments listed below are the recommended actions staff members should take when handling rule violations.

Staff members should not create their own punishments or change punishments without proper approval.

When determining punishment, consider:

- The severity of the offense
- The impact on roleplay
- Whether the player has repeated the offense

Repeated offenses may result in increased punishment, including moving from a warning to a kick or ban.

### Minor Offenses

- Unrealistic Avatar
  - Punishment: Verbal Warning
  - A warning should be given if an avatar is extremely unrealistic and negatively affects roleplay.

- Lying to Staff
  - Punishment: Warning
  - Players must provide truthful information when speaking with staff members.

- Glitching
  - Punishment: Warning
  - If glitching heavily disrupts roleplay, staff may issue a temporary kick.

- Fail Roleplay (FRP)
  - Punishment: Warning
  - Includes failing to properly participate in roleplay situations, including Fear Roleplay violations.

- Breaking New Life Rule (NLR)
  - Punishment: Warning

- Tool Abuse
  - Punishment: Warning; Jail time if necessary

- Staff or Player Disrespect
  - Punishment: Warning
  - Respect is required between all members of the community.

- Staff Command Abuse (!mod)
  - Punishment: Warning

- Random Death Match (RDM)
  - Punishment: Warning; Jail time

- Vehicle Death Match (VDM)
  - Punishment: Warning; Jail time

### Major Offenses

- Staff Evasion
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Respawning to Avoid Arrest
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Spawn Killing / Safe Zone RDM
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Leaving to Avoid Arrest
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Impersonating Staff
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Harassment
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Cuff Rush / Auto Jailing
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

- Disrupting Convoys
  - Punishment: Kick for 30 minutes

### Extreme Offenses

Extreme offenses are serious violations that can severely impact the community.

- Exploiting
  - Punishment: Ban

- Extremist Behavior
  - Punishment: Ban

- Racism
  - Punishment: Ban

- NSFW Actions
  - Punishment: Ban

- Banned Roleplay
  - Punishment: Ban

- Leaving to Avoid Punishment (LTAP)
  - Punishment: Ban

- Joining Before Kick Timer Ends
  - Punishment: Ban

## Punishment Documentation

Before issuing any punishment, staff members must inform the player of what rule they violated and what punishment they are receiving.

Use:

:pm (username) (reason) [punishment and duration]

Example:

:pm Player123 RDM [Warning + Jail Time]

Players should always understand why action was taken against them.

If a punishment is issued without proper explanation, it may be considered invalid unless it involves an extreme offense.

## Staff Duty Requirements

Staff members may begin moderating when the server has at least:

- 5 active players

To begin your staff shift:

1. Join the ARP game server.
2. Join the ARP Office (Sheriff Team).
3. Equip your staff uniform through the wardrobe.
4. Wear the uniform assigned to your rank.
5. Log into Melonly using your Discord account connected to ARP.
6. Navigate to: Dashboard → Army Roleplay → Moderator Panel
7. Select: Start Shift

## Shift Abuse Policy

Staff members are expected to actively moderate while on shift.

Shift grinding is prohibited.

Examples include:

- Leaving your shift active after leaving the game
- Remaining AFK while on duty
- Abusing the break feature
- Keeping shifts active without actually moderating

Remember: being on duty means you are representing ARP.
Maintain professionalism, proper grammar, and respectful communication.

## Roleplay Operations

### Presidential Roleplay Guidelines

Staff members with proper authority may host Presidential Roleplay events.
These events should be organized, realistic, and enjoyable for everyone involved.

#### Presidential Roles

- President — The player who receives the highest number of votes.
- Vice President — The player who receives the second highest number of votes.
- Secret Service (SS) — Responsible for protecting the President.
- Counter Assault Team (CAT) — Specialized security personnel responsible for responding to threats.
- Civilians — Players participating as general citizens.
- Mafia — Organized civilian groups attempting to target the President.

#### Election Procedures

Presidential elections should be hosted in the:

- Police Department Meeting Room

Staff should prepare the area before beginning.

Recommended setup:

- Three voting areas
- Police barricades or crowd fences
- Peace Timer: 500 seconds
- Only use objects that improve the roleplay experience.

#### Election Announcements

Use:

:h Presidential elections are being hosted in the police department meeting room!

Repeat announcements approximately every minute until enough players arrive.
The :m command should only be used when necessary.

#### Candidate Process

Announce:

:h Thank you all for attending this election. I will now be selecting three candidates.

Select three candidates and place them into voting areas.
Candidates may give speeches before voting begins.
Voting announcement:

:h Voting has started! Please stand in the box of the candidate you are voting for.

The candidate with the most votes becomes President.
The candidate with the second most votes becomes Vice President.
Staff should record usernames to prevent confusion.

#### Presidential Convoy Procedures

Presidential convoys must remain organized and realistic.

Requirements:

- Convoy stays in one organized line
- Staff vehicle leads the convoy
- Civilians cannot interfere with the convoy
- No standard Liberty County preset liveries
- Minimum of one limousine or SUV replacement

During movement:

- No unnecessary overtaking
- No honking
- No ramming
- No unnecessary sirens
- Roads around the convoy should be secured.

##### Convoy Speed

Maintain:

- 15-30 MPH or 15-30 KM/H

A Peace Timer is recommended during convoy movement.

#### Presidential Protection

The President may travel anywhere that provides:

- A realistic environment
- Fair opportunities for roleplay
- Proper security coverage

The first destination should always be:

- The White House (Hospital / ICU)

Staff should regularly announce:

- President location
- Vice President location
- Remaining lives

#### Presidential Death Procedures

When the President is eliminated:

- Set a reasonable Peace Timer.
- Restore the President if applicable.
- Consider remaining lives.

The maximum Peace Timer should be:

- 3 minutes

Once the President loses all lives:

- The Vice President takes control.
- Staff may restart the scenario.
- Staff may vote on the next roleplay event.

Communication between staff members is required.
